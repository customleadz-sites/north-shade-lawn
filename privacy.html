Privacy Policy

How North Shade Lawn LLC collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us.

Last updated: August 14, 2026

North Shade Lawn LLC (“North Shade Lawn,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates this website at northshadelawnllc.com. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. If anything here is unclear, call or text us at (989) 763-3899 and we’ll answer plainly.

Information you give us

When you fill out the quote-request form on our Contact page, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • The city where the property is located
  • Whatever project details you choose to type into the message box

You only give us this information if you choose to submit the form. If you’d rather not, you can simply call, text, or email us instead.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, ours collects some basic technical information as you browse, using Google Analytics:

  • Pages you viewed and how long you stayed
  • The website, search engine, or ad that sent you here
  • General location (city or region — not your street address)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system

This is aggregated, anonymous data used to understand which pages are useful. We do not use it to identify you personally.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and schedule an estimate
  • To follow up about a project you asked about
  • To provide the services you hire us for
  • To improve how the website works

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not add you to a marketing list without you asking for it.
  • We do not collect payment or credit card details through this website.

Who else touches your information

A few trusted service providers handle data on our behalf, strictly so the site can function:

  • Web3Forms — delivers the contact-form submission to our email inbox.
  • Google Analytics — anonymous website traffic statistics.
  • Google Maps — the embedded map on our contact page.
  • Vercel — hosts this website and keeps standard server logs.

We may also share information if the law requires it — for example, in response to a valid legal request.

Cookies

Google Analytics places a small file (a “cookie”) in your browser to tell repeat visits apart from new ones. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work normally.

Text messages and calls

If you give us your phone number, you’re agreeing that we may call or text you about your request or project. We don’t send promotional mass texts. Reply STOP at any time and we’ll stop texting.

How long we keep it

Quote requests stay in our email records so we have a history of the work we’ve discussed and completed. Ask us to delete yours and we will.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and form submissions are transmitted securely. No system is perfect, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you send us.

Children

This website is meant for adults hiring a landscaping company. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you what information we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Email northshadelawn23@gmail.com or call (989) 763-3899.

Links to other sites

Our site links out to places like Instagram and Facebook. Once you leave northshadelawnllc.com, their privacy policies apply, not ours.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top.
